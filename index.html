 The VIP Wristband / Movie Ticket Analogy

              

              

                
Traditional servers try to remember every user's face, which crashes when millions visit. Instead, Spring Boot REST APIs use a **JWT (JSON Web Token)**. It's like a stamped paper wristband. Once logged in, the server gives you a wristband with your seat number and name. For all future actions, you just flash the wristband! The guard just checks the signature stamp without looking you up in a database.

 Caching Metaphor (The Dining Table Fruit Bowl)

              

              

                
When you want an apple, driving to the farm (Database) every single time takes hours. Instead, you put a **Fruit Bowl (Cache)** right on your kitchen counter. If the apple is in the bowl, you grab it instantly (Cache Hit). Only if the bowl is empty do you drive to the farm (Cache Miss), fetch it, and refill the bowl!
